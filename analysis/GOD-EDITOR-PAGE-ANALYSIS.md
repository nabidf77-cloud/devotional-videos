## 3. Content Formula (the winning template)

- **Visuals:** Hyper-realistic **AI-generated / AI-animated Hindu deity art** — Durga, Lakshmi,
  Ganesha, Hanuman, Vishnu, Krishna–Radha, Shiva, Parvati, Kartik. Ornate jewelry, flowers,
  dramatic lighting. Recent videos are AI-animated (Veo watermark); older ones were static images.
- **Duration:** **15–32 seconds** (most 0:15–0:24).
- **Caption structure:** hook = 🌹🙏🏻 → body = short inspirational English news paragraph →
  **NO explicit CTA** (no "share/comment" ask in the viral-era template).
- **Recurring elements:** 🌹🙏🏻 opener; older eras used hashtags (#maalaxmi #ai #viral #trending
  #shorts #video #fbreels #reels) and emoji-only captions (😳😳, 🙏🙏).
- **Themes:** devotional AI deity visuals + one fixed inspirational news story.
- **What performs best:** short AI-animated deity videos + the news caption. Photo posts never
  exceed ~200 likes; videos range 500–793K likes. Durga / Lakshmi / Ganesha dominate the top.

## 5. Before vs After the 19 Apr 2026 Inflection

| | Before (photo era) | After (viral era) |
|---|---|---|
| Format | Static AI images as **photo posts** | **15–32s AI-animated** deity reels (Veo) |
| Caption | Hindi devotional, some share-bait ("write Jai Mata Di, share to 3 people") | **English emotional news story** + 🌹🙏🏻 hook, no CTA |
| Engagement | 25–95 likes | 300–6,000 immediately → 100K–793K within weeks |

**The 4 changes at the jump:** (1) photo → short reel, (2) Hindi devotional caption → English
inspirational news story, (3) static image → AI-animated video, (4) ~60s → ~15–24s duration.
**The reach trigger = devotional visuals + inspirational news caption pairing.** The same
visuals as photo posts never took off.

## 6. Posting Pattern & Audience

- **Frequency:** ~3–4 posts/day (Sep–Oct 2026), mixing videos with occasional photo posts.
- **Timing:** videos cluster **morning (06:00–10:00)** and **evening (18:00–19:00) IST**.
- **Comments:** purely devotional Hindi/Hinglish — "Jai Mata Di", "Har Har Mahadev",

## 7. Key Takeaways

1. **The format switch is everything:** 12 silent months → photos flopped → short reels exploded 100x overnight.
2. **Caption ≠ devotional text:** an *inspirational news story* outperforms devotional captions.
3. **No CTA needed:** the viral template has zero share/comment ask — the content itself drives shares.
4. **Animation > static:** AI-animated (Veo) beats static AI images decisively.
5. **Short wins:** 15–24s is the sweet spot; 60s+ videos from 2025 never broke 700 likes.
6. **Consistency compounds:** 3–4 posts/day at fixed morning/evening slots built the base from 3K to 27K+ per post.
